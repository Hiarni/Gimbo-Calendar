# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to the Gimbo Kalender app

## In short

Gimbo Kalender stores your data only on your device. There is no account, no own server, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The developer receives no data from you.

## Controller

Hiarni · Contact via [github.com/Hiarni](https://github.com/Hiarni)

## Data on your device

- Events, calendars, categories, tasks, notes, templates and attachment copies are stored in the app on your device.
- Android app backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app deletes the data.
- Backups (JSON, ZIP) and exports (ICS, VTODO) are only created when you start them. They are unencrypted and saved where you choose.

## Permissions

All permissions are optional and only needed for the respective feature:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications, exact alarms, device start | Reminders for events and tasks, also after a restart |
| Calendar, sync settings | Show, edit and synchronise account calendars |
| Contacts | One-time birthday import |
| Approximate location | Determine the place for weather |
| Internet | Optional online services (see below) |

## Optional online services

These services are only contacted when you explicitly use the feature. The providers see your IP address and technical request data.

| Service | Data sent |
|---|---|
| Nager.Date (public holidays) | Country and year |
| OpenHolidays API (school holidays) | Country, year, region and school group |
| OpenStreetMap Nominatim (place search) | Search term and language |
| MET Norway (weather) | Coordinates |

Contacts, attachments and full event texts are never sent automatically.

## Account calendars and other apps

Account calendars (for example Google, Microsoft, CalDAV) are synchronised by the calendar provider on your Android device. Maps, meeting links and sharing open other apps with the selected content. The privacy policies of the respective providers apply.

## Your rights

As the developer does not process any personal data about you, all data stays in your hands: you can change, export or delete it in the app at any time. For data held by the online services listed above, contact the respective provider. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

## Changes

This policy will be updated when the app changes. The current version in this repository applies.
